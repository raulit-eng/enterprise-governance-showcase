## 🔍 Methodology: Forensic Profiling & Backward-Design

Most data cleanup treats symptoms in place and risks silent data loss. Every engagement in this practice follows a **Forensic Backward-Design Methodology**:

1. **Define the Clean Target End-State:** Start from the exact import-ready schema, reconciled financial ledger, or executive KPI view required at the finish line.
2. **Preserve the Scene First (Immutable Staging):** Treat incoming client files like a forensic scene—never modify raw inputs in place. Every engagement begins by sealing an untouched copy with a **SHA-256 Chain-of-Custody** fingerprint.
3. **Trace Root Causes Backward:** Identify *why* and *where* records broke—catching exact/fuzzy duplicates, cross-column math mismatches (`Qty × Price ≠ Total`), hidden entry typos (`$5` vs. `$500`), and shifted columns.
4. **Deliver Clean Data + Transparent Audit Proof:** Return an executive-formatted, import-ready Excel/CSV workbook alongside a dedicated quarantine report documenting every merged, corrected, or flagged row.

## 🔒 The 4-Pillar Client Protection Guarantee

* **1. Zero-Shortcut, Row-by-Row Inspection:** Automated deterministic rules inspect every row across deduplication, formatting standardization, and cross-column math—nothing is guessed or silently deleted.
* **2. Immutable Backup & Always-Available Vault:** Original client files remain untouched inside an isolated, timestamped vault verified with **SHA-256 cryptographic hashing**.
* **3. Side-by-Side Proof & Quarantine Reporting:** Clients receive a clean, easy-to-follow master sheet plus a dedicated discrepancy tab detailing every flagged or removed record.
* **4. Zero-Data-Loss Rollback & Replay:** All transformations execute on isolated copies so any edge case can be diffed against the sealed original and restored with zero data loss.

## 🏗️ Defensive Architecture & Governance Standards

*Note: Core automation engines remain in private repositories to protect proprietary intellectual property. Below is the architectural blueprint governing all client engagements:*

* **4-Layer Defensive Security Firewall:**
  * **Layer 1 — Immutable Forensic Staging:** Isolated intake directories with SHA-256 intake and delivery manifests.
  * **Layer 2 — PCI / PII Detection & Luhn Redaction:** Automated scanning and masking of sensitive payment card numbers (Luhn-validated) and personal identifiers prior to reporting.

  * **Clean Verified Deliverable (`.xlsx` / `.csv`):** Executive-styled, schema-validated dataset ready for immediate CRM, POS, or accounting import.
